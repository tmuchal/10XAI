Opponent scouting report

Every trait is measured against the other teams in your data. Every recommendation shows the number behind it.

Player ratings

Ratings run 1–99: 50 is the average player in the same position line in your data, and every 15 points is one standard deviation. Match ratings run 1–10, where 6.5 is an average game. Soccer: click a player for the full scouting profile.

Match plan

Picks the XI and formation that maximise your objective against this opponent, then pre-plans substitutions for common game states. Effects are damped (±25% max on expected goals), so treat gains under 0.03 as a tie.

Live substitution planner

Set the minute and score, and tick who's on the pitch. Every possible change (and pairs of changes) is scored on the same objective. Players tire according to their measured late-game drop in success rate.

Import real matches: StatsBomb open data

Free event data for full competitions (World Cups, Euros, some league seasons): every pass, shot with xG, duel and substitution, with pitch location. Licensed for non-commercial use with attribution. Pick one team to import only its matches, or leave it blank for the whole competition (capped at 80).

Tag a match from video

Open the video tagger: it plays the YouTube video in an embedded player (no download) while you log each action with a hotkey and a click on the pitch. The saved match feeds straight into ratings, scouting and tactics.

YouTube match intake

Metadata comes from YouTube's official oEmbed endpoint. Downloading video or captions breaks YouTube's Terms of Service without the rights holder's permission, so those steps stop at the gate for manual approval.

Commentary → events

Paste a transcript. Each action is credited to the nearest roster player named before it. Commentary only covers headline actions, so passing and defending stay thin.
