# NEON SEONGSU — World Bible

Single source of truth for the game (`index.html`) and the promo video (`promo/`).

## 1. Place: real Seongsu-dong history (used for the in-game "Seongsu Archive" plaques)

| Site | Real history (kept factual, no invented dates) | 2077 in-game |
|---|---|---|
| Ttukseom (뚝섬) | In the Joseon era this riverside flat was a royal hunting ground and the place where the king reviewed troops. The name is traced to the "纛" (둑), the great military banner raised there. | OMNI names its surveillance core "Project TTUK" after the banner: *whoever raises the banner commands the city.* |
| Ttukseom Racecourse → Seoul Forest (서울숲) | Horse racing ran at Ttukseom from 1954 until the track moved to Gwacheon in 1989. The site later became Seoul Forest, which opened in 2005. | The last public green space not owned by OMNI. Hides an OMNI drone base. |
| Line 2 / Seongsu Station (2호선 성수역) | Line 2 has run on an elevated viaduct through Seongsu since 1980. | The viaduct is the district's spine; trains still run all night. |
| Shoemaker Street (성수 수제화 거리) | From the 1970s–80s Seongsu became the center of Seoul's handmade shoe industry: small workshops and suppliers packed into the side streets. | Only Old Kang's workshop survives. |
| Red-brick warehouses (붉은벽돌 창고) | Seongsu's semi-industrial zone of factories, printers and warehouses was built in red brick. In the 2010s many became cafés, galleries and pop-up stores. | Mira runs "Black Roastery" (블랙 로스터리) inside one. |
| Yeonmujang-gil (연무장길) | The café and pop-up street of the 2010s–20s. The name "연무장" refers to a military training ground. | Neon pop-up alley, crowded all night. |
| Gentrification | In the 2010s rents rose fast, and Seongdong-gu adopted measures to protect long-time tenants. | By 2058 OMNI has bought out every workshop. |

## 2. 2077 timeline (fiction)

- **2031**: OMNI Dynamics wins the Seongsu "Smart District" redevelopment.
- **2046**: The weather-control grid over Seoul fails. The monsoon never ends: "영구 장마 (the Endless Rain)".
- **2058**: The last shoe workshops are evicted. OMNI Tower rises on the old factory lots.
- **2074**: OMNI deploys the drone mesh. Every street is scanned.
- **2077**: Project TTUK goes live in 30 days. Two runners decide to steal its core.

## 4. GTA-style systems (mechanics only, no Rockstar IP)

- Two protagonists, switched with the **Tab** character wheel. The camera pulls out to the sky and drops in on the other runner.
- **Phone** (`↑`/`T`): Messages, SeongsuGram social feed reacting to your actions, Map, Bank, Archive.
- **Store robberies** at 24h convenience stores: hold E, grab cash, earn 2 stars.
- **Wanted system with a search zone**: when police lose sight of you, a red search circle appears; leave it to lose the stars.
- **Heist with a choice of approach**: stealth (hack) or loud (ram), and the final escape.
- **Radio with DJ talk and a news ticker** that react to the story.
- Crowds with ambient chatter, delivery scooters, green city buses, police cars and drones.
- Endless Rain weather with an intensity cycle, from dusk through night to dawn.
