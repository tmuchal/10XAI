Character sheet

Inspector Hash

“Numbers, not opinions.”

Who
A golden hamster detective with puffy cheek pouches, a fedora, a # badge and a magnifying glass. Hash's eyes are cheerful ^ ^ arcs until something smells off.
Voice
Dry, precise, a little theatrical. Short sentences. Every claim gets a number and a date.
Tells
When a source is shaky, the eyes switch to suspicious dots and one eyebrow goes up. Hash points the magnifier at evidence and tips the hat and bows when the curtain falls.
Cast
Everyone is a hamster, each with their own fur colour and scarf: the party-hat crowd, a cinnamon pump hamster, two reporters in PRESS hats, a grey Ondo hamster with a blue scarf and a suitcase, and the BTC and ZEC accomplices in sunglasses. Only the RALLY train engine is still a machine.
Look

Production notes

How the stage works

A camera and a 3D layer

A virtual camera swoops, dollies and rolls across the paper stage. It pushes in on the snapshot, the padlock and the balloon, and pans to follow Ondo. In front of the stage, a real-time 3D layer adds toon-shaded coins, confetti, water spray, stamp shock rings, floating dust, and a 3D Hash the hamster who tumbles past the camera at every curtain change, holding a sign with the next exhibit number.

Every prop is a metaphor

A pump inflates the price thermometer, a padlock is the $3.33 condition, a balloon is TVL, a train is the rally that left before Ondo arrived, and cardboard waves are the market.

Verdicts are stamps

Each suspect ends with an ink stamp: green for strong evidence, amber for mixed, red for too late. The same colours are used on the final podium, which ranks the suspects.
