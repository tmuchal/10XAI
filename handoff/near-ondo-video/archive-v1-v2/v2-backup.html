Production notes

How the stage works

One set, many props

The curtains, painted backdrop and wooden floor never change. The curtains close between exhibits and the props swap behind them, like a real puppet theatre.

Every prop is a metaphor

A pump inflates the price thermometer, a padlock is the $3.33 condition, a balloon is TVL, a train is the rally that left before Ondo arrived, and cardboard waves are the market.

Verdicts are stamps

Each suspect ends with an ink stamp: green for strong evidence, amber for mixed, red for too late. The same colours are used on the final podium, which ranks the suspects.
