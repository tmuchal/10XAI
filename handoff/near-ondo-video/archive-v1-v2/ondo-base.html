Character sheet

Uchay

“Numbers, not opinions.”

Who
A detective in a fuzzy red hooded suit with round ear pods and two bobbing antennae. The face is painted green with pale streaks under the eyes, and the mouth drops into a big shocked "O" whenever there's a number worth shouting about.
Voice
Dry, precise, a little theatrical. Short sentences. Every claim gets a number and a date.
Tells
Wide, startled eyes at rest. When a source is shaky the eyes narrow and a brow drops. Uchay points the magnifier at evidence, and the antennae wobble and bob for the curtain-call bow.
Cast
Everyone is a hamster, each with their own fur colour and scarf: the party-hat crowd, a cinnamon pump hamster, two reporters in PRESS hats, a grey Ondo hamster with a blue scarf and a suitcase, and the BTC and ZEC accomplices in sunglasses. Only the RALLY train engine is still a machine.
Look
Watercolor wash on grainy paper with thick brown-black ink lines. The lines "boil" (redraw slightly, 8 times a second) like hand-drawn cel animation.

Production notes

How the stage works

A camera and a 3D layer

A virtual camera swoops, dollies and rolls across the paper stage. It pushes in on the snapshot, the padlock and the balloon, and pans to follow Ondo. In front of the stage, a real-time 3D layer adds toon-shaded coins, confetti, water spray, stamp shock rings, floating dust, and a 3D Uchay who tumbles past the camera at every curtain change, holding a sign with the next exhibit number.

Every prop is a metaphor

A pump inflates the price thermometer, a padlock is the $3.33 condition, a balloon is TVL, a train is the rally that left before Ondo arrived, and cardboard waves are the market.

Verdicts are stamps

Each suspect ends with an ink stamp: green for strong evidence, amber for mixed, red for too late. The same colours are used on the final podium, which ranks the suspects.
