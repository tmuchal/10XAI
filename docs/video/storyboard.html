Animation rules

  • Characters animate on 2s (12 fps). Their outlines "boil" through three wobble frames. The animatic player on this page shows the effect.
  • Backgrounds are painted once per set: day stage, night stage, Kanban wall. They never boil or move, except for the camera.
  • All outlines use one warm brown ink (#3A2418), never black, at about 3px for a 960px-wide frame. Characters get one soft watercolor highlight and no gradients.
  • Every gag lands on a beat. The three big hits are the pumps (S04), the bell (S09) and the approval stamp (S16).
  • Only Dr. Harness speaks real words. The boxes chatter in "뽀뽀" gibberish, with subtitles carrying the meaning.
  • On-screen numbers (72 in S04, 78 in S16, $0.40 · 12 min in S17) are placeholders. Replace them with the numbers from your own dry run before final animation (CLAUDE.md rule 5: claimed vs. measured).

Production plan

Five passes, from locked script to final export. The frames on this page are layered vector SVG, so they can be imported directly as a rough first pass.

  1. Lock the script and record a scratch VORecord Dr. Harness's Korean lines at a calm, slightly amused pace. The 20 lines total about 2:10 of speech, which leaves room for gags and music.
  2. Cut the animaticScreen-record "Play animatic" on this page, lay the scratch VO and temp music under it, and fix the timing before anything gets drawn.
  3. Paint the sets and build the rigsPaint three watercolor plates (day stage, night stage, Kanban wall) in Procreate or Krita with a wet brush plus a dry ink liner. Rig the six characters in After Effects or Rive: body, face, legs, arm nubs and hat on separate layers.
  4. Animate on 2s and add the boilKey the gag beats first (S04 pumps, S09 bell, S16 stamp), then do the in-betweens. Add the three-frame line boil at the end.
  5. Mix, subtitle and exportUkulele, toy piano and kazoo at about 100 BPM. Burn in the Korean subtitles and export the English SRT. Deliver 16:9 at 1920×1080, plus three Shorts re-staged for 9:16 from S04, S16 and S17.
