Why this works

  • 8–10 slides. Ten slides tells one story in one sitting, and Instagram now allows up to 20 per carousel, so there is room without padding.
  • Cover hook of 15 Korean characters or fewer. "'무료'라며? 직접 재봤어요" is 15 characters with spaces and punctuation (9 Hangul syllables). The cover works as the thumbnail, the way 고구마팜 treats it.
  • Mascot-led voice. Dr. Harness carries every slide, in the spirit of 뉴닉's explainer mascot. He even peeks in from the right edge as the swipe cue.
  • Native Korean lines. The Korean copy is written in short 해요체 sentences in the 뉴닉/캐릿 tone, with no translationese, and uses terms the way Korean creators do (에이전트, 칸반, 샌드박스, 하네스).
  • Big type, one tip per slide. The Ruben Hassid pattern: a 30–44 px (logical) headline, then a single visual.
  • Step-by-step flow. Slides 5 to 8 follow the board left to right, the way ByteByteGo draws a pipeline.
  • Save/share CTA. The last slide asks for a save and a send to one specific teammate, which gives the reader a reason to act.
  • 4:5 with a 3:4 grid-safe center. The profile grid crops 4:5 posts to 3:4, so all key content sits in the central 505 logical px.

Export

The PNGs live in docs/video/cardnews/ as slide-01.png to slide-10.png, each 1080×1350. To rebuild them from the repo root:

NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node docs/video/tools/render-cardnews.cjs

The script opens this page in headless Chromium, reads window.CARD_SLIDES, and screenshots each slide alone on a 540×675 page at device scale 2. It waits for Gaegu, Gowun Dodum and IBM Plex Mono and prints whether they loaded. If Google Fonts is unreachable, the PNGs fall back to system fonts, so re-run it on your own machine before posting.

Upload order is slide 1 to 10. Every measured number is marked as an example on the slide; replace them with your dry-run results first.

3D cover variant

The video formats are Blender 3D motion graphics. For the carousel, the cover (slide 1) and the curtain call (slide 10) can later be swapped for Blender hero renders of Dr. Harness and the board. Keep the same 4:5 frame, the header band, and the headline panel position, so the carousel still reads as one series. Slides 2 to 9 stay as 2D watercolor.
