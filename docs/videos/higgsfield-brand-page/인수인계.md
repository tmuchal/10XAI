## 2. Reference videos / images and structure analysis

### 2.1 Style reference — X post by @other_reality ("Claude Opus 5.5 has the best visual design of any model I've tested")
A ~2–3 min animated short made in code. Structure / look:
- **Puppet-theater stage**: red velvet curtains + tie-backs, scalloped valance, wooden floor, warm watercolor paper backdrop with soft sunburst.
- **Characters**: boxy orange characters with ^_^ faces and stubby legs, plus a small scientist figure; all with thick dark ink outlines and flat colors.
- **Big physical props as the explanation** (e.g. a pump inflating a "P(DOOM)" thermometer) instead of UI screens.
- **Captions**: hand-lettered text in a dark navy rounded box at the bottom ("I'm upping my P(doom)").
- Motion: stop-motion-like line boil, bouncy squash-and-stretch, one gag per beat.
Our film adopted: stage/curtains/floor, watercolor paper, ink outlines, navy caption box, handwritten font (Gaegu), line boil, curtain transitions between chapters, party-hat extras.

### 2.2 Character-sheet reference (user image: man in black suit, 3 panels on grey)
- Panel 1: **full body front, cropped below the chin — face NOT shown**, shoes in frame.
- Panel 2: **full body back**, same crop and pose.
- Panel 3: **large face close-up** (~45 % of the width), front-on, face + shoulders.
- Plain seamless grey background. Matches the Higgsfield Cinema/Soul Cinema character-sheet standard (full-body front, full-body back, close-up headshot).
